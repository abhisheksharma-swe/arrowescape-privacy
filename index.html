Arrow Escape: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Arrow Escape ("the app") is published by Techweta ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (such as completed levels, sound and vibration preferences) are saved locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

This version of the app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking services. We do not share any data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, by adding ads), we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.
